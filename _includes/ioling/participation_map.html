{% comment %} IOL participation map. Nothing here needs updating each year: the three categories are derived from contests.yml (hosts) and results_by_countries.yml (participation years). Adding the next contest + its results recolours the map automatically. Categories are mutually exclusive, in priority order: host -- has hosted an IOL at least once current -- competed in the most recent IOL (but has never hosted) past -- competed in an earlier IOL only endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign current = current | append: " " -%} {%- assign past = past | append: " " -%} {%- assign last_pairs = last_pairs | split: " " -%} {%- comment -%} Tooltip detail per
